Beechcraft King Air 350 Checklist

Before Starting Engines:
- Parking Brake
- Battery Switch
- Avionics Master Switch
- Fuel Quantity
- Fuel Selectors
- Generator Switches
- Prop Levers
- Power Levers
- Condition Levers

Engine Start:
- Left Condition Lever
- Left Starter Button
- Left ITT
- Left Ng
- Left Generator
- Right Condition Lever
- Right Starter Button
- Right ITT
- Right Ng
- Right Generator
- Inverter Switch

Before Taxi:
- Flaps
- Flight Instruments
- Altimeter
- Heading Indicator
- NAV Radios
- Autopilot
- Trims
- Pressurization

Taxi:
1. Brakes
2. Instruments
3. Nosewheel Steering
4. Heading Indicator

Before Takeoff:
1. Seats, Belts, Harnesses
2. Doors and Windows
3. Flight Controls
4. Trim
5. Flaps
6. Prop Levers
7. Condition Levers
8. Autofeather
9. Anti-Ice
10. Pitot Heat
11. Transponder
12. Lights

Takeoff:
1. Power Levers
2. Airspeed
3. Rotate
4. Positive Rate / Gear Up
5. Climb Power

Climb:
- Power
- Prop RPM
- Airspeed
- Pressurization
- Engine Instruments

Cruise:
- Power Setting
- Prop RPM
- Condition Levers
- Pressurization
- Fuel Balance

Descent:
- Pressurization
- Altimeter
- Airspeed
- Anti-Ice

Before Landing:
- Seats, Belts, Harnesses
- Props
- Gear
- Flaps
- Airspeed

After Landing:
1. Flaps
2. Props
3. Condition Levers
4. Anti-Ice
5. Transponder
6. Landing / Taxi Lights

Shutdown:
1. Parking Brake
2. Avionics
3. Condition Levers
4. Battery
5. Fuel Selectors
